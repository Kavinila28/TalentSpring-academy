NEED TO KNOW?

Frequently Asked Questions

Some helpful details before you apply.

Who can apply?

Students interested in learning and developing practical skills can submit an application. Eligibility and selection depend on the specific program.

Are the internships online?

The learning initiative is designed for virtual participation. Specific schedules, instructions, and requirements will be communicated for each program.

Is there an application fee?

There is no application fee charged by this website. Any future optional paid services, if introduced, should have their costs and terms clearly disclosed before purchase.

Will I receive a certificate?

Certificate eligibility and any applicable conditions will be confirmed in the official details for the particular program. Submitting an application alone does not guarantee a certificate.

Does applying guarantee selection or a job?

No. Submitting an application does not guarantee selection, employment, a stipend, placement, or a job offer.

How do I apply?

Click any Apply for this internship button. It opens the TalentSpring Academy Google Form, where you can submit your application.
