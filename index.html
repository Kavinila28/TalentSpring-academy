ABOUT TALENTSPRING

Small steps. Meaningful growth.

TalentSpring Academy is being developed to help college students explore practical skills through structured, project-based virtual learning experiences.

Our approach is built around clear tasks, honest assessment, feedback, and work that students can document in a portfolio.

  • Defined tasks and learning outcomes
  • Transparent selection and completion criteria
  • Respect for student privacy and original work
  • No promises of jobs, stipends, or accreditation without a genuine basis

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Are the internships free?

The sample pilot described in this starter is planned as free. Confirm the final fees, dates and program details before accepting applications. Any future paid service must be clearly disclosed in advance.

Will I receive a certificate?

Eligible participants should receive a standard completion certificate at no additional charge after meeting the published requirements. A certificate documents completion; it does not guarantee employment or accreditation.

Is this a job or a guaranteed placement?

No. The programs described here are educational, project-based experiences unless a specific, verified employment arrangement is clearly stated.

How do I submit tasks?

Task submission must be configured by the program administrator. This demo provides a local dashboard mockup; it does not securely transmit submissions to the founder.

How is my application information used?

Before launch, publish a privacy notice explaining what data you collect, why you collect it, who can access it, how long it is retained, and how applicants can request correction or deletion.
